What is Exquisite Corpse?

Exquisite Corpse is a collaborative drawing and writing game that was originally developed by Surrealist artists in the early 20th century, most notably André Breton and Yves Tanguy. The game is designed to be a creative, spontaneous, and often humorous way for multiple participants to collectively create an artwork or piece of writing.

The name "exquisite corpse" is said to have originated from the first phrase created in the game, which was "The exquisite corpse shall drink the new wine." This phrase was created by the Surrealist poets during one of their early sessions of the game.
